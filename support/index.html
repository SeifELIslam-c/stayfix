Help topics

These are the most common support requests handled for StayFix users and App Store review.

Sign in with Apple

We support Sign in with Apple for account creation and returning users. Contact us if Apple sign-in fails, is cancelled, or does not open the expected account.

Google and email login

We can help with Google sign-in, email/password access, password reset, and account recovery questions.

Account deletion

Users can request or complete account deletion from the app's Privacy and Account Center. Support can help if you cannot access the app.

Privacy requests

Contact us for questions about profile information, account records, app permissions, or data handling.

Bug reports

Report crashes, blocked screens, sign-in errors, missing data, messaging issues, or workflow problems.

App review access

Apple App Review can use the credentials provided in App Store Connect and contact support if access does not work during review.

Privacy and safety

StayFix support helps with privacy questions, account removal, and safe app access.

Data access

You can contact us to ask what account information is connected to your StayFix profile.

Delete account

Open StayFix, go to your profile, then Privacy and Account Center. If you cannot sign in, email support and request account deletion help.

Report abuse

Report unwanted behavior, unsafe content, or app misuse through the app or by emailing support.
