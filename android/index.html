Privacy Policy

Stayfix Job - Staff Application

1. Introduction

Welcome to Stayfix Job. This privacy policy explains how we handle the professional data of hotel staff to ensure a transparent and efficient connection with hotel management.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with our features, we collect the following professional data:

  • Profile & Resume: Your personal details, skills, and uploaded CV/Resume.
  • Work Availability: The specific hours and days you declare as available for work.
  • Attendance Records: Time and date stamps when you perform Check-in and Check-out actions within the app.

3. Data Sharing & Usage

Your data is strictly used to facilitate your employment and daily tasks. Your Profile, CV, Availability, and Attendance data are shared directly and exclusively with the Hotel Directors/Managers who use the primary Stayfix Management App.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for secure authentication and database management. Your data is stored securely in the cloud to ensure real-time synchronization with the management team.

5. Data Security & Rights

We take your privacy seriously. You have the right to update your CV, modify your availability, or request account deletion through the app settings or by contacting your hotel administration.
