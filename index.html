Help topics

Sign in with Apple

We support Apple sign-in for new accounts and returning users. Contact support if Apple sign-in fails or opens the wrong account.

Google and email login

We can help with Google sign-in, email/password access, password reset, and account recovery.

Account deletion

Users can delete accounts from the app Privacy and Account Center. If you cannot sign in, contact support for deletion help.

Privacy requests

Contact support for questions about profile data, account records, app permissions, and data handling.

Bug reports

Report crashes, blocked screens, sign-in errors, missing data, messaging issues, or workflow problems.

App Store Review

Apple App Review can use the credentials provided in App Store Connect and contact support if access does not work during review.
